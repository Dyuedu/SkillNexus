# Competitive Research Digest: Vietnam IT Job Platforms & Career Advisory

## Competitor 1: ITviec (itviec.com)
- Focus: #1 IT-specific job board. Acquired by Mynavi (Japan) in 2019.
- Products: Job postings, TalentConnect, Employer Branding, Company Reviews, IT Insider Salary Report.
- Gaps: No candidate-facing skill gap tool, no AI CV optimization for candidates, no career graph. AI matching is recruiter-side only. Heavily biased towards Mid/Senior roles; freshers find few openings.

## Competitor 2: TopCV (topcv.vn)
- Focus: Vietnam's largest CV & general recruitment platform.
- Products: CV Builder (best-in-class in VN), Toppy AI (CV scoring against generic standards, smart job matching), SHiring ATS.
- Gaps: No graph-structured ontology; Toppy AI does not diagnose gaps against specific target role skill graphs. High user frustration with tele-marketing spam after posting public CVs.

## Competitor 3: VietnamWorks (vietnamworks.com / Navigos Group)
- Focus: Vietnam's largest general job board (acquired by en Japan). Sub-brand: VietnamWorks inTECH.
- Products: AI screening for recruiters (Kanban match scoring), applicant match percentage, AMS.
- Gaps: Match score is recruiter-facing screening, not candidate career advisory. Frequent complaints about candidate ghosting and stale/ghost jobs.

## Competitor 4: TopDev (topdev.vn)
- Focus: Developer community + IT job board. ~$1M+ strategic investment from Saramin HR (Korea).
- Products: CV Convert, Smart Job Matching, Vietnam IT & Tech Talent Landscape Report.
- Gaps: Treated as a media/content + job board rather than an interactive career intelligence engine.

## Competitor 5: LinkedIn (Vietnam)
- Focus: 5M+ members in VN. Strong for MNCs, senior tech, FDI hiring.
- Features: Economic Graph, LinkedIn Learning, Premium Career ($39.99/mo).
- Gaps: USD pricing is prohibitive for typical VN developers. Not localized for VN IT salary norms, outsourcing career ladders, or BrSE paths.

## Competitor 6: CareerViet (careerviet.vn / formerly CareerBuilder VN)
- Features: CareerMap (static decision tree), VietnamSalary.
- Gaps: Decision tree is static, not updated dynamically from real-time market signals; generalist focus.

## Competitor 7: Emerging Entrants (UpNow.vn, etc.)
- UpNow: Conversational profile building, AI match score, stated career roadmap feature. Early-stage, vector-based (no verified GraphRAG/knowledge graph).

## Key Whitespace
1. No Vietnam-localized skill-to-career-path knowledge graph.
2. No interactive, personalized skill gap diagnostic ("Missing these 3 skills for Senior Java; 3-month roadmap").
3. No real-time market intelligence queries for developers ("Why is Golang salary 20% higher than Java this quarter?").
4. No trusted company intelligence from developer perspective (OT culture, real tech stack, ghosting rate).
