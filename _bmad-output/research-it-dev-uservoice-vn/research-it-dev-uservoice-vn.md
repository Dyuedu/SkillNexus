# User-Voice Research Digest: Vietnamese IT Developers & Students

## 1. Segments & Core Jobs-To-Be-Done (JTBD)
- Fresher (0–1 yr): "Get my first real job with a real codebase; stop sending blank CVs."
- Junior (1–2 yrs): "Stop getting rejected; diagnose exactly what skills I'm missing."
- Mid-level (2–4 yrs): "Switch to a product company / MNC for a 15–25% salary bump; break into AI/cloud."
- All levels: "Know what a company's real OT culture and tech stack are like before applying."

## 2. Complaint & Workaround Patterns
- Ghosting epidemic: 70–80% of candidates report submitting dozens of CVs with zero reply ("bị bơ", "chìm xuống đáy biển").
- JD Inflation: Fresher roles requiring Docker, K8s, AWS, microservices, and 2 years commercial experience ("Senior hóa Fresher").
- Ghost Jobs: Open job listings maintained for PR or talent pooling with no active headcount.
- Salary "Negotiable" ("Thương lượng"): Major waste of time; developers demand upfront transparent salary bands (Gross & Net).
- Workarounds: Mass CV spamming ("Rải CV"), asking in private Telegram/Facebook groups for OT reviews, using ChatGPT to tailor CV keywords against JDs.

## 3. Delight & Switching Triggers
- ITviec's "Ít nhưng mà chất" won users over VietnamWorks' "nồi lẩu" due to IT-only focus and salary transparency.
- Community GitHub repos (e.g. `awesome-jobs/vietnam`) gained traction solely by enforcing mandatory salary brackets and no-agency rules.
- Willingness to pay: Vietnamese devs will pay for income-linked tools ($3–$8/mo) if they provide verified salary reality, automated CV tailoring against specific JDs, and a concrete learning roadmap.

## 4. Top 5 Actionable Product Insights
1. **Salary Reality as Core Value Prop**: Transparent salary brackets (Gross/Net/Tax calculator built-in).
2. **Skill Gap -> Roadmap Engine**: Interactive "Current CV vs Target Role" with 30-60-90 day learning milestones.
3. **Company Reputation & Responsiveness Scoring**: Score companies on recruiter reply rate, actual OT culture, real tech stack.
4. **Intern-to-Fresher Pipeline**: Focus on the highest-probability entry funnel for students/graduates.
5. **AI/Cloud Upskilling Tracks**: Bridge the gap from CRUD developer to high-value AI/Cloud roles.
