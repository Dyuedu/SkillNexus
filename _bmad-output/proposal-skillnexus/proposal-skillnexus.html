01 Executive summary

Vietnamese IT job seekers decide what to learn, what to ask for and how to write their CV with little real evidence. Job posts are inflated and often hide the salary, and the market reports come out once a year as PDFs. AI CV tools rewrite text against generic standards, not against what Vietnamese employers actually ask for.

SkillNexus is a data platform plus an AI layer. A daily pipeline collects and normalizes Vietnamese IT job data, annual salary reports and open datasets into a warehouse where every figure carries its source, period and sample size. On top of that, local LLM workflows tell a user:

  • which skills the market wants for their target role;
  • what those skills are worth;
  • how much of the demand their CV already covers;
  • what to learn next, in what order, within their weekly time;
  • how to tailor their CV to a specific job post without inventing experience.

The project is designed to teach a complete data engineering workflow (Airflow, a medallion warehouse, dbt, a graph projection), real data science (co-occurrence analysis, a job-post inflation index, salary-premium regression, trend momentum), and applied AI (LLM-in-ETL extraction, a guarded agent workflow, a tool-using market analyst with evaluation sets). It still addresses a problem people would pay to solve.

04 Proposed solution

Four modules form one user journey. Each module reads from the same gold tables, so the market numbers a user sees in module 1 are the same ones that rank their gaps in module 3.

M1 · Market Pulse

"What does the market want?"
  • Skill demand by role × level × city
  • Skill bundles (co-occurrence graph)
  • Job-post inflation index
  • Salary transparency index
  • Ask-the-market agent with citations

M2 · Trends & Salary

"What is rising? What am I worth?"
  • Digitized annual reports → momentum (rising / flat / falling)
  • Salary range for a profile
  • Salary premium per skill (with CI)
  • Real vs nominal wages (CPI)

M3 · My Gap & Plan

"How far am I, and what next?"
  • Market-coverage score of my CV
  • Ranked missing skills
  • Ordered roadmap within my hours per week
  • Capstone project + free resources

M4 · My CV

"How do I present it for this post?"
  • CV tailored to a specific post
  • Batch tailoring for saved posts
  • Claim check: nothing the CV can't back up
  • Diff view with reasons

Design principles

  1. Math computes, the LLM narrates. Every number comes from SQL, a graph query or a model; the LLM explains it and writes text.
  2. Provenance on every number: source · period · sample size · confidence. Values with n < 30 are hidden by default.
  3. No fake trends. Trend lines only connect points from the same source series; no numeric forecasts in the MVP.
  4. Honest scores. "Covers 62% of skill demand across 412 Junior Backend posts", never "62% chance of being hired".
  5. Privacy first. CVs are processed by a local LLM only and never mixed into shared market data.

Key research finding: VietJobs is often described as "48k IT jobs", but it contains only 1,906 IT-category rows and about 794 real software/data posts. That is enough for a seed set and a baseline, not for role × level × city statistics. So the light live feed is on the critical path, and the job-detail fact extraction depends on a terms-of-use sign-off (gate G1, end of week 1). If G1 is refused, we fall back to user-contributed posts plus an optional licensed API (jobspipe.dev, ≈ $20/month) and narrow the scope of market statistics.

07 Scope

IN · MVP

  • Daily pipeline: sitemaps → job facts → silver → gold → graph
  • Backfill: VietJobs, 4 ITviec reports, TopDev reports, SO survey, CPI/FX
  • M1: demand, bundles, inflation index, transparency, market agent
  • M2: report momentum, salary range & skill premium, real wages
  • M3: coverage score, ranked gaps, time-budgeted roadmap
  • M4: JD-tailored CV with claim check (+ batch)
  • Evaluation harness with 4 gold sets
  • PDPL controls: consent, local processing, deletion

STRETCH

  • Job-post decoder (must-have vs nice-to-have, red flags)
  • Emerging-skill detector
  • Hidden-salary imputation vs. the VietJobs paper baseline
  • Weekly market newsletter; curriculum-gap report (B2B)

OUT

  • Numeric forecasts (need ≥ 12 monthly points)
  • Ghost-job detection (needs months of sightings)
  • Company reviews / OT culture (legal risk)
  • Auto-applying to jobs (terms-of-use and licensing risk)
  • Mock-interview agent, payments, mobile app

12 Legal, privacy & ethics

  • Personal data (PDPL, Law 91/2025/QH15, in force since 1 Jan 2026): explicit, unbundled consent per upload; CVs processed only by a local LLM, so there's no cross-border transfer; raw files deleted within 24 h; one-click full deletion; user data never enters the shared market dataset.
  • Third-party job content: the sites' robots.txt files allow job pages, and we only fetch what they permit, at a low rate with an honest user-agent. We store extracted facts, not copied text, and purge raw pages after 30 days. Use is non-commercial and academic; a commercial launch would require partnerships or a licensed feed. TopDev's terms forbid copying and redistributing content; ITviec's terms are still to be reviewed (gate G1).
  • Reports and open data: every figure taken from ITviec or TopDev reports is cited per edition. Stack Overflow data follows ODbL attribution. The VietJobs dataset license is to be confirmed.
  • Employment-service licensing: SkillNexus does not place candidates or forward applications, so it stays an analytics tool, not a licensed recruitment intermediary.
  • Fairness & honesty: no hiring-probability claims; confidence labels; recommendations never encourage misrepresentation.
