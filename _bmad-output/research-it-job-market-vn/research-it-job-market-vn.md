# Market Research Digest: Vietnam IT Job Market & Career Advisory Platform Opportunity 2024–2026

## Dimension 1: Market Size & Growth

- Vietnam ICT revenue: ~$165.9B (2024), reaching ~$198B (2025), ~8-12% of GDP.
- Tech workforce: ~560,000 IT professionals in Vietnam (2024), ~74,000–80,000 ICT enterprises.
- Pipeline: ~55,000–57,000 new IT/CS graduates annually (TopDev). Demand projected at 750k–3M by 2030.
- Salary bands: Freshers: $400–$900/month; Mid-level: $900–$2,000/month; Senior: $2,000–$4,000+/month.
- Demand growth: AI/ML, Cloud, Data Engineering roles growing 20–30% YoY.
- TAM/SOM Proxy: Addressing 100k IT professionals/students at EdTech ARPU ~$42.69/year yields ~$4.3M ARR. Broad B2C career tool TAM: ~$15–50M ARR.

## Dimension 2: Customer Segments & Behavior

- Average developer age: ~29 years old.
- 93%+ IT professionals already integrate AI tools (ChatGPT, Copilot) daily.
- #1 job switching driver: Compensation transparency (34%+).
- Strong demand for clear career pathing (Mid -> Senior/Lead, Web -> AI/Cloud) rather than generic job listings.

## Dimension 3: Pain Points & Unmet Needs

- Structural quality gap: Only ~30% of 55k graduates are immediately job-ready by employer standards.
- Ghosting & opaque requirements: Entry/mid devs send dozens of CVs without replies; vague JDs with "salary negotiable".
- No existing Vietnam-native GraphRAG/Knowledge Graph platform that bridges market signals to individual CV gap analysis.

## Dimension 4: Competitive Landscape

- ITviec, TopCV, VietnamWorks, TopDev: Dominated by job board / employer branding models. None offer personalized interactive career advisory or GraphRAG skill gap analysis.
- Absence of B2C career intelligence products tailored to Vietnam tech careers.

## Dimension 5: GTM & Pricing Dynamics

- WTP for career/income-linked tools: 79,000–199,000 VND/month ($3–$8/mo) is the sweet spot.
- Payment methods: MoMo, ZaloPay, VietQR are mandatory.
- GTM channel: Developer communities (Viblo, Facebook tech groups, University CS clubs).
